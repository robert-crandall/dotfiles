Run a grilling session, using the `/domain-modeling` skill.

Write every question in **ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English**: one idea per sentence, short sentences, active voice, and the same word for the same thing every time. Where the project has a `CONTEXT.md`, use its ubiquitous language for domain terms. Define any other term at first use, or pick a plainer one. And give every question a little bit of context, give me a little bit of context, assume I do not have access to your thinking logs.
